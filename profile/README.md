# 1986 AG

> Seek the Unique.

1986 AG is a Zurich-based independent supercar company behind the long-established Autowelt Schweiz brand. We source, trade, and advise on rare supercars, hypercars, classics, and limited-edition collector vehicles.

## What We Do

| Area | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| Collector Cars | Rare supercars, hypercars, classics, and limited editions |
| Sourcing | Global search, brokerage, and off-market opportunities |
| Advisory | Portfolio guidance for private collectors and enthusiasts |
| Services | Trade-ins, financing, factory orders, and international logistics |
| Experience | A private showroom and Carlounge in Zurich |

## Brands We Work With

Porsche, Ferrari, Bugatti, Pagani, Koenigsegg, Aston Martin, Lamborghini, McLaren, Bentley, Rolls-Royce, Mercedes-Benz, Mercedes-AMG, and HOF.
